StoryTeller

The home of YT Stories — original stories, told aloud.

YT Stories is a personal YouTube channel of original narration: crime stories, stories to sleep to, old-fashioned fairytales, and tales of the future. Every story is written for this channel.

Visit the channel: youtube.com/@youtubestories-d7p

StoryTeller is also the name of the private, single-user tool the channel owner uses to upload videos to this channel through YouTube's official API. It is not distributed, has no other users, and accesses only the owner's own account. Its privacy practices are described on the privacy page.
