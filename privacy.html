Privacy Policy — StoryTeller

Last updated: October 26, 2026

StoryTeller is a private, single-user application. It is used by one person (the owner of the YT Stories YouTube channel) to upload that person's own videos to that person's own channel using the official YouTube Data API.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the application accesses the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and check their processing status.

What it does not do

  • It has no other users. Nobody else can sign in or be signed in.
  • It does not collect, store, sell, or share any other person's data.
  • It does not access email, contacts, files, or any other Google service.
  • Access is via an OAuth token that the owner can revoke at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately ends the application's access.
